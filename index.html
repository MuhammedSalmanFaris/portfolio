About Me

I'm Salman Faris, a Unity game developer. Since January 2025 I've been working remotely as a Unity Game Developer (C#) for WGC, a Korea-based gaming company, on Tetrominos Battle, a Tetris-style mobile game. My work covers gameplay features, game UI and the story dialogue system.

I started making games as a hobby, teaching myself by building small projects and experimenting with gameplay mechanics, and that hobby became my career. I enjoy turning ideas into polished, playable experiences, from game feel and controls to clean, responsive game UI, and I keep learning to push my work further.
